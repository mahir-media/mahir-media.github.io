Questions, answered.

What is a bespoke website?

A website designed and coded from scratch for one business, with no template underneath. Every layout, colour and interaction is chosen for your brand, so it can't be mistaken for anyone else's.

Who is Mahir Media Studio for?

Businesses and startups in Kolkata and across India that want a website that stands out instead of blending in.

Will I own the website and its code?

Yes. We hand over the full source code, connect your domain and walk you through it, so you can edit it yourself or move it to any developer later.

Can you add scroll animation and 3D?

Yes, where it earns attention. Motion and interactive 3D are part of what we design, and we keep sites fast and usable on phones.

Where is Mahir Media Studio based?

Kolkata, West Bengal, India. The studio is founded and run by Sourav Burman.
